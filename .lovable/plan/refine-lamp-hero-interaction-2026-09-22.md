# Refine Lamp Hero Interaction

## Scope
Update only the existing homepage opening sequence. Keep the current lamp asset, typography, dark aesthetic, and final left/right composition.

## Changes
- Give the centered lamp a subtle load-in from dim to faintly visible, never an empty black opening.
- Restrict activation to clicking, tapping, Enter, or Space on the lamp itself; add a restrained desktop hover cue.
- Stage the sequence as lamp illumination, graceful center-to-left movement, warm ambience reaching the text, progressive text reveal, then “GO”.
- Add a soft, irregular, diffused warm ambient field behind the text without a visible spotlight, beam, circle, rectangle, or neon bloom.
- Keep most of the viewport dark and preserve the responsive mobile composition.
- Verify initial and final states on desktop and mobile, including lamp centering and lamp-only activation.
